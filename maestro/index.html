How to conduct
  1. Watch the lane at the top. Bounce your baton down and up each time an orb reaches the ring. Start on a Ready orb, and the orchestra joins on Go!
  2. Big orbs want big, loud beats. Small orbs want small, soft ones.
  3. A colored bar that says Lift to Brass means: raise your baton into the brass band above the orchestra and hold it there.
  4. Not sure? Copy the ghost baton on stage. It shows when to beat, how big, and where to point.
  5. On the last orb, hold the final chord, then beat once more on Cut!

Public-domain scores, played by an orchestra synthesized live in your browser. No recordings, nothing to license. Keyboard works too: Space beats, ↑ ↓ louder and softer, 1–4 cue a section.
