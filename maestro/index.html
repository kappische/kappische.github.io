How to conduct
  1. Your baton is the ball on the left. Keep it on the gold line as it scrolls in, like Trombone Champ.
  2. The line bounces once per beat, and the bottom of each bounce is the beat. After two Ready bounces the orchestra starts on Go!
  3. The orchestra plays on its own, but its mood follows you. Conduct well and it plays together, in tune and full. Slack off and it gets sloppy.
  4. Tall bounces are loud, small ones soft. Beat a little ahead or behind to push the tempo.
  5. When the line shoots up into the cue zone with a section's name, reach up with it to bring them in. At the end the line goes flat: hold, then dip once on Cut!

Public-domain scores, played by an orchestra synthesized live in your browser. No recordings, nothing to license. Keyboard works too: Space beats, ↑ ↓ louder and softer, 1–4 cue a section.
