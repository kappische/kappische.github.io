How to conduct
  1. Give one preparation beat. The orchestra starts on your second beat, at the speed you set.
  2. Bounce once per beat: down, then up. Stop beating and the orchestra waits for you.
  3. Bigger bounces play louder. Match the dynamics on the score, from a small p to a huge ff.
  4. When a section glows, lift the baton into the band above it and hold for a moment to bring it in.
  5. At the end, hold the final chord, then give one more beat to cut it off.

Public-domain scores, played by an orchestra synthesized live in your browser. No recordings, nothing to license. Keyboard works too: Space beats, ↑ ↓ louder and softer, 1–4 cue a section.
